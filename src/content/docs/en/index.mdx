  <p>
    <strong>ORION：</strong> 
    A prominent constellation located on the celestial equator. Boasting numerous high-luminosity stars, distinct nebulae (such as M42), and its iconic "Orion's Belt," it is recognized as one of the most structurally stable and easily observable constellations in the night sky. In ancient navigation, rather than offering vague direction, it served as a <strong>core baseline</strong> for determining celestial coordinates and spatial boundaries.
